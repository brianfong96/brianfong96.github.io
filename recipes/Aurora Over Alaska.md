# Aurora Over Alaska

An Alaska-inspired Northern Lights mocktail first, with a gin cocktail variation. The flavor profile should be northern forest and berry: boreal botanicals, birch, lingonberry, lemon, and butterfly pea. The finished drink should show glacier blue, emerald green, violet, and pink ribbons through crushed ice.

This is Alaska-inspired rather than a traditional Alaskan recipe.

## Prep the bases

### Butterfly pea concentrate

Steep:

- 10–12 dried butterfly pea flowers
- 3 oz / 90 mL hot water

Steep 8–10 minutes, strain, and chill completely.

The tea should be very dark cobalt blue because the final drink and ice will dilute it.

### Lingonberry pour

Because the D'Arbo product is a sauce rather than a syrup:

- 1 tbsp lingonberry sauce
- 1 tbsp hot water

Mix until smooth, then fine-strain and chill.

### Emerald dilution

Mix:

- 1 tiny drop green food coloring
- 2 tbsp / 30 mL cold water

This keeps the green translucent and prevents one concentrated drop from coloring the entire drink.

## Four aurora colors per drink

### Glacier blue

- 1/4 oz / 7.5 mL chilled butterfly pea concentrate

### Emerald

- 1/4 oz / 7.5 mL diluted green water

### Violet

- 1/4 oz / 7.5 mL butterfly pea concentrate
- 3–5 drops lemon juice

### Aurora pink

- 1 tsp strained lingonberry pour
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp butterfly pea concentrate

Butterfly pea is pH-sensitive, so lemon shifts the blue pigment toward violet and pink. Exact hues vary with tea strength and acidity.

## Mocktail

Per drink:

- 1/2 oz / 15 mL KWE Boreal & Hops tonic syrup
- 1 tsp / 5 mL birch syrup
- 1/4 oz / 7.5 mL fresh lemon juice
- 3 oz / 90 mL plain soda water
- tiny knife-tip pinch silver edible shimmer
- four aurora colors above
- crushed or pebble ice

### Build

1. Chill a clear Collins or highball glass.
2. Fill it completely with crushed or pebble ice.
3. In a small mixing glass, combine KWE syrup, birch syrup, and lemon juice.
4. Stir briefly with ice.
5. Strain over the fresh crushed ice.
6. Slowly add 3 oz soda water.
7. Add a very small pinch of edible silver shimmer.
8. Rotate the glass once so the shimmer creates subtle moving currents.
9. Add the four aurora colors according to the presentation sequence below.
10. Do not stir before serving.
11. Give the drink one gentle stir before actually drinking it.

Taste target: lightly sweet, tart, botanical, slightly bitter.

If too sweet, add more soda. If too bitter, add about 1/2 tsp extra birch syrup.

## Northern Lights presentation

Use this order:

1. **Blue first:** slowly pour the glacier-blue concentrate down one inside wall of the glass.
2. **Green opposite:** pour the emerald dilution down the opposite side.
3. **Violet:** drizzle through several pieces of ice in the upper middle.
4. **Pink last:** use a teaspoon, pipette, straw, or squeeze bottle to create 2–3 thin pink streaks in separate ice channels.
5. Stop mixing.

The goal is irregular vertical ribbons and clouds, not horizontal layered stripes.

Visual tips:

- Clear ice works better than cloudy ice.
- Fill the glass completely with crushed ice to slow color mixing.
- Serve immediately because the colors will gradually merge toward blue-purple.
- Dark backgrounds or side lighting make the colors and shimmer much stronger.
- Garnish minimally with a lemon twist, frozen blueberry, or small rosemary sprig.
- Use only food-grade edible shimmer.
- Less shimmer is better.

## Cocktail variation

Per drink:

- 1 1/2 oz / 45 mL juniper-forward gin
- 1/4 oz / 7.5 mL KWE Boreal & Hops tonic syrup
- 1 tsp / 5 mL birch syrup
- 1/4 oz / 7.5 mL fresh lemon juice
- 1 1/2 oz / 45 mL plain soda water
- same shimmer
- same four color ribbons

### Build

1. Stir the gin, KWE syrup, birch syrup, and lemon juice with ice for about 10 seconds.
2. Strain into a clear highball packed with fresh crushed or pebble ice.
3. Top with 1 1/2 oz soda water.
4. Add the tiny pinch of shimmer.
5. Add blue, green, violet, and pink in the same order as the mocktail.
6. Serve unstirred for presentation.
7. Stir gently once before drinking.

Gin contributes juniper and dryness. Keeping the KWE portion at 1/4 oz avoids overpowering the drink with hops and quinine.

## Flavor explanation



- **KWE Boreal & Hops:** northern forest botanicals, hops, lime, tonic bitterness
- **Birch syrup:** concentrated tree-sap sweetness, caramel, woody depth
- **Lingonberry:** tart northern berry character
- **Lemon:** acidity and butterfly-pea color change
- **Butterfly pea:** visual color system with mild tea flavor
- **Gin:** juniper and botanical structure
- **Silver shimmer:** visual only

The guiding principle:

**Forest first. Color second.**

The colored additions should stay small so this tastes like a proper botanical drink rather than colored syrup.
