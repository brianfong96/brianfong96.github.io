# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Curious technology professionals who want clear, evidence-led explanations of technical, financial, and project topics.

## Product Purpose

Brian Fong's personal site publishes projects, technical learnings, and original writing. The finance section helps readers understand company performance through accurate, source-backed, side-by-side comparisons that make dense financial data easier to digest.

## Positioning

Finance articles go beyond static tables by normalizing misleading headline figures and pairing the analysis with interactive visual explanations, including flow diagrams that show where cash is generated and spent.

## Operating Context

Readers browse the public website on desktop and mobile, moving between concise summaries, company comparisons, visualizations, methodology notes, and primary-source citations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Finance coverage must compare the named companies side by side, preserve metric definitions and caveats, remain usable without a build step, and work through the site's existing navigation and SPA-style transitions. New work is reviewed on a local server before any push or merge.

## Brand Commitments

The site uses Brian Fong's name and direct first-person voice. The dark green terminal identity remains part of the broader site and projects, while the blog uses a distinct editorial research-desk identity. Finance analysis uses navy and blue structure, green positive values, and red negative values.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied Q2 2026 comparison covers Alphabet, Meta, Microsoft, and Amazon across revenue, operating profit, normalized earnings, cloud performance, operating cash flow, capital expenditures, and free cash flow. It includes links to each company's investor-relations release and identifies non-operating gains and unusual expenses that affect comparability.

## Product Principles

- Make comparisons fair before making them dramatic.
- Let visuals reveal the movement and scale behind the numbers.
- Keep sources and methodology close to every consequential claim.
- Preserve a clear reading path on both desktop and mobile.
- Prefer useful interpretation over financial spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Interactive visuals must retain equivalent text or tabular explanations, keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion support.
