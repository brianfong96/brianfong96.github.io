What each company proved

The ranking changes depending on whether the question is financial quality, growth, cloud acceleration, or revenue momentum.

  1. Financial quality

    Microsoft

    Highest operating margin, highest operating cash flow, and by far the strongest quarterly FCF.

  2. Growth + economics

    Alphabet

    24% revenue growth, 82% Google Cloud growth, and cloud profit more than tripling—tempered by a doubled capex bill.

  3. Cloud profit acceleration

    Amazon

    AWS operating income grew 64%, but negative quarterly and trailing FCF raise the burden of proof.

  4. Revenue momentum

    Meta

    Fastest consolidated growth at 28%, but infrastructure absorbed almost all operating cash generation.

Methodology

How free cash flow was compared

Quarterly FCF is operating cash flow minus the capex measure each company uses in its own FCF presentation. Meta includes finance-lease principal payments. Amazon deducts property purchases net of sales and incentives. Microsoft and Alphabet use cash purchases of property and equipment.

Why cloud revenue is not perfectly comparable

AWS and Google Cloud disclose direct segment revenue. Microsoft's Intelligent Cloud segment includes server products and enterprise services, while the broader Microsoft Cloud measure includes products outside Azure. Azure growth is therefore shown without an invented revenue estimate.

How unusual items were handled

Alphabet and Amazon underlying-profit figures are approximations based on disclosed investment gains and reported tax information. Microsoft's adjusted EPS subtracts the disclosed $0.27 benefit. Meta's add-back illustrates the effect of legal and severance charges but does not erase those costs.

How market moves and valuation multiples were calculated

Post-earnings moves compare the July 30, 2026 regular-session close with July 29. Trailing P/E divides the July 30 close by reported trailing diluted EPS. P/FCF uses trailing free cash flow per share. Values are rounded to one decimal place and will differ from live quote services after the snapshot date.
