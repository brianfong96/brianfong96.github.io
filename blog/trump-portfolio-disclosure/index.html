Methodology and limits

The static extract preserves the source snapshot's aggregate evidence while keeping the post small enough for GitHub Pages.

What is inside the extract

Substantive Part 6 stock, preferred-stock, and equity-ETF positions are included with matched Part 7 purchase and sale activity. Bond, commodity, cash, mutual-fund, corporate-debt, income-only, and de minimis rows at or below $1,001 are outside this extract.

Why all dollar figures remain ranges

OGE disclosures report statutory value and transaction bands. Adding lower and upper bounds gives an envelope, not an appraisal. No exact shares, execution quantities, execution times, or execution prices are available.

How the source validation reconciles

The source parsed 21,285 Part 7 rows, matched 19,417, and published 19,407 events. The checked-in implementation does not explain the 10-row difference between matched and published totals, so this post states the gap rather than assuming those rows were equivalent.

What changes over time

Filing ranges, dates, pages, rows, and accounts are fixed to this source snapshot. Prices, returns, reference closes, and history coverage are generated market context frozen through July 10, 2026.
